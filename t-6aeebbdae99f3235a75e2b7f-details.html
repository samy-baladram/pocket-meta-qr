Phase 2

Top Cut

In this single-elimination phase, you will play Best-of-3 (BO3) matches against matched opponents.

Losing a round eliminates you from the bracket.

Victorious players advance through the bracket until a champion is determined.

⚠️ Rules ⚠️

By registering and participating, you agree to follow all stated rules and accept the consequences of violations.

🛡️ GENERAL

ᛃ Harassment, hate speech, or offensive remarks (homophobia, transphobia, racism) will result in reprecaussians depending on the severity of the attack.

ᛃ Do not argue with opponents; call a judge if an issue arises.

ᛃ Play fairly and maintain sportsmanlike conduct.

ᛃ Staff reserve the right to disqualify or ban players for rule violations or destructive and suspicious behaviour.

📜 Decklist Submissions

ᛃ Your submitted decklist must match the exact cards played throughout the event, this includes the energy that you are using.

ᛃ Cosmetic variations (Full Art, Rainbow Rare, etc.) are allowed if gameplay effects are identical.

ᛃ Submitting cards with different attacks/abilities than the cards played in rounds results in a Game Loss and forced deck correction.

ᛃ If your opponent plays a card that is not listed in their decklist, take a screenshot and call a judge immediately.

⏱️ CHECK-INS

ᛃ Failing to check in before the timer expires results in a Game Loss.

ᛃ If your opponent isn't checked in but is active in chat, proceed with your game.

ᛃ If both players check in but one is unresponsive, call a judge before reporting any score.

ᛃ Persistent slow play across rounds may result in disqualification following staff review.

ᛃ Aim to have a timer interval of 3 minutes to the least, anything drastically higher may result in a game loss

⚔️ During The Match

ᛃ Mid-game disconnections result in a Game Loss for the disconnected player.

ᛃ Turn-0 wins are void—screenshot the screen and call a judge to confirm.

ᛃ Accidental concessions or launching with the wrong deck count as a Game Loss.

ᛃ If you notice that you are playing the wrong decklist before any cards have been placed down and exposed, take a screenshot to prove that it was infact an accident and reset the match with the correct deck. (You only have one chance to do this, making the same mistake twice will lead to Game Losses)

ᛃ Create a new match code with your opponent if the default code fails.

ᛃ If an opponent isnt searching or searching becomes an issue, take screenshots of the error WITH TIMESTAMPS (The time on your device shown) so that you can have evidence for potential judge calls

ᛃ Keep your client updated. Playing on an outdated version from Round 3 onward results in an automatic Game Loss.

📊 WIN / LOSS / TIE REPORTS

ᛃ Report all match outcomes accurately on Limitless.

ᛃ If a mistake occurs, call a judge to correct the match score.

ᛃ Capture victory screen screenshots as proof when requested.

ᛃ False match reports will result in an immediate disqualification.

ᛃ Intentional ties (ID) are permitted—call a judge to log an ID.

📊 TIES DURING BO3 MATCHES

ᛃ If game 1 results in a tie, both players recieve a win and proceed straight to game 3.

ᛃ If game 2 results in a tie, the player who won game 1 takes the win for the match.

ᛃ if game 3 results in a tie, if both players agree AND if there is time left to do so, players may play a sudden death game (first in-game point wins)

ᛃ If there is no time remaining to play a sudden death or if thre is no agreement, the result for the match will be a tie for both players.

⚖️ Judge Calls

ᛃ Only call a judge when necessary to resolve active disputes.

ᛃ Spamming Judge requests without reason may lead to warnings and possible disqualification from the tournament.

ᛃ English is preferred for judge communications to keep calls efficient.

ᛃ Screenshots or video proof are required for judges to resolve disputes accureatly.

ᛃ Treat all staff respectfully; disrespectful behavior will lead to disqualification.

ᛃ Please be patient while waiting for a judge, if it has been a while you are welcome to ping judges within the Discord server

ᛃ Join the Discord server to submit match evidence when prompted within the "Judge-Evidence" channel.

📺 Twitch Casting

ᛃ Feature matches are optional, including top-cut rounds unless stated otherwise.

ᛃ Stream sniping will result in immediate disqualification and a permanent ban from The Dark League Tournaments.

ᛃ Maintain respectful conduct while featured on live streams.

ᛃ When playing on stream, join the designated Discord voice channel and remain muted/deafened.

💰 Prize Pool

ᛃ Contact @moonlightshurikenVT on Discord to claim winning payouts.

ᛃ Ensure that you are a member of The Dark League Discord Server to be able to claim your prize

ᛃ Card trades require at least 25,000 Stardust in-game.

ᛃ Cash payouts are processed via PayPal or Venmo.

ᛃ Winners are responsible for covering any transaction/transfer fees.

ᛃ Unclaimed prizes will be rolled over into future community prize pools.
