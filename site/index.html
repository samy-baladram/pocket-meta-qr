About Pocket Meta QR

The decks that win in Pokémon TCG Pocket tournaments right now, the latest events with their top 8, and a QR code for every list, so you can import a deck in a few taps.

How do I import a deck?

Open a deck, tap its QR code and save the image. In the game: My Decks → + → Scan Code → Scan Saved Image. On a computer, you can also point the game’s camera at the screen.

Where does the data come from?

Public Standard events with 8 or more players on Limitless TCG, from the last 7 days, because the meta changes fast (up to 14 days when there are few events, late in a set; never before the set’s release). The numbers are computed here, so they can differ a little from other sites.

What is Limitless TCG?

Limitless TCG is the main place where Pokémon TCG Pocket tournaments are played online. New events start there every day, from small cups to open events with more than 300 players, and thousands of players take part every week. Each result is public as soon as an event ends, and this site checks for new ones every hour, so the meta here is days fresher than a tier list made by hand. Pocket Meta QR is not part of Limitless TCG: it reads its public results.

Why Limitless TCG, and can I trust the results?

Because every number comes from real tournament games, recorded while the event runs. Each event shows its standings and the result of every match in every round, and players hand in their deck list before the event starts, so each list is the deck that was really played. On top of that, this site counts only public Standard events with 8 or more players and 1 deck per player, and only real matches: no byes, no mirror matches.

Which days count?

The last 7 days. When there are few events (often late in a set) the page uses the last 10 or 14 days, so every number has enough players behind it. It never counts events from before the current set’s release, so in a new set’s first days the results are early ones. The line under the title shows the dates.

What is in Latest Events?

The tournaments of the same days, newest first. Open one to see the lists of its top 8 players, with a QR code for each. Events where players bring more than 1 deck are not listed, because they have no single list per player.

What is a “top player”?

A player with at least 2 more wins than losses at a single-deck event.

What do “Unproven” and “No top result” mean?

The deck has no top player in these days. Unproven: some players won more than they lost, but none reached a top result; the page shows the best of them. No top result: no player won more than they lost; the page shows the best-placed list. Both are a starting point, not a proven list.

How are the lists chosen?

List 1 of a deck is the list that the most top players used. A list with only 1, 2 or 3 cards swapped counts with it: the tiles under the list show how many top players used it exactly or with 1, 2 or 3 swaps (tap a tile for those versions, each with its own QR code). A list with 4 or more cards swapped is shown as its own list when 2 or more top players used it, or 1 player finished in the top 8 of an event with 32 or more players. ★ Popular now marks a list 1 that clearly more top players used exactly than any other list.

Why is the win rate different from other sites?

Mirror matches (the same deck on both sides) and events where players bring more than 1 deck are not counted. A tie counts as half a win.

Can I use it like an app?

Yes: tap Add to Home Screen at the bottom of the page for the steps.

What does this site not do?

It shows only the current meta, the decks, lists and events of the last 7 to 14 days, so it does not keep a history, list every card or build decks.

  • No older weeks or past sets: no results or tier lists from before the days shown under the title.
  • No deck history: the 7-day trend in a deck’s stats is the longest view.
  • Not every card: the Packs tab and the card pages show only the cards that this week’s meta decks play.
  • No deck builder: import a list with its QR code, then change it in the game.
  • Not every tournament: only public Standard events with 8 or more players and 1 deck per player.

This is on purpose, so every number on the page is about the game as it is played today. For past results and every Pokémon TCG Pocket card, Limitless TCG keeps the full archive.

Is it free?

Yes: no ads and no sign-up. No cookies and no personal data: the site only counts visits and button taps, anonymously (GoatCounter). This browser keeps only your theme and the last tab you used.
